What this settles

The gesture is an accelerator; the header action is the contract. Both call the same current-scope refresh, and neither owns business data.

Axis lock and threshold

The first movement past 8 px decides the axis for the whole contact. A horizontal contact is surrendered to the Sessions/Workspace pager and the pull never arms.

A vertical contact at the list top arms the pull with 0.5 resistance, capped at 96 px. Release commits once at ≥ 64 px of raw travel; anything shorter cancels with no request.

Guards

Not at top → ordinary scrolling, no indicator. Nested collection scroller → its own scroll. Terminal → the surface owns its touches. The platform edge guard and the shell's edge band keep their pixels.

Visible alternative

The header Refresh is present in healthy, empty and failed states, is keyboard operable, and reports busy only until its own promise settles. It is not a permanent refresh row and it does not duplicate the header.

Fidelity gaps

Rows, latency and failure are a deterministic mock. There is no WebSocket, no pull controller, no production token pipeline and no real Capsule measurement. Chromium touch emulation only — no Safari or physical-device claim. The 64 px threshold and 0.5 resistance are initial proposals to tune by observation.
