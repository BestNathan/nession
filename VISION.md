# Nession Vision

Nession is an intelligent workspace for working continuously across devices, environments, and compute nodes.

Modern work is increasingly fragmented across local machines, remote servers, cloud environments, sandboxes, terminals, coding agents, and other tools. Users should not have to constantly rebuild context or manually manage the infrastructure boundaries between them.

Nession aims to provide a unified, continuous, and extensible working experience across these environments.

The underlying infrastructure may be distributed and complex, but the product experience should remain coherent and simple. Nession should absorb infrastructure complexity and preserve the user's working context, allowing work to move naturally between local and remote environments, devices, tools, and compute resources.

Nession's long-term direction is to become an intelligent workspace where distributed execution, remote access, contextual tools, and AI-assisted workflows feel like parts of one continuous environment rather than separate systems.
