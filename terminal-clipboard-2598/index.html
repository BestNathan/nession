Transient clipboard states

OSC 52 text copy and OSC 5522 MIME paste stay quiet when they work. These are the only states the surface adds: a user-gesture copy when the browser blocks an async write, a refused-write notice, and one honest sentence when a TUI has not negotiated the image protocol. No permanent paste/upload toolbar.
